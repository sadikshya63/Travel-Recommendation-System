Travel Safety Tips

  • Carry a copy of your identification and important travel documents.
  • Keep emergency contact numbers saved on your phone.
  • Stay updated on weather conditions before trekking or traveling to remote areas.
  • Use trusted transportation and hotel services whenever possible.
  • Inform family or friends about your travel route when going on long trips.

MeroYatra provides travel information, but for urgent help always contact local authorities or emergency services directly.
